Method. Each item's daily figure is the median unit price of every matched product that day — not an average, so one mispriced listing can't move the line. n is how many products backed it.

Tiers. Tier A is scraped live from a named public source, with the URL as the receipt — every basket item, and the licensed-exchange dollar. Tier B is a published institutional figure, cited with its source — the official exchange rate. Tier C (human market reports) is not on this page yet, and will never be mixed silently into the others.

Raw data. Every day's rows are committed unmodified to the public archive — append-only, timestamped, and never edited after the fact. Check our arithmetic.

How these numbers are made

Each price is a median, not an average — one mispriced listing can't move it. Listings is how many it came from, and you can open any row to see them.

We price the staple pack, not the sachet. Where pack sizes vary, the larger half is used — a bag of rice, not a 500g pouch. Listings outside a published plausibility range are rejected before the median is taken, and the number rejected is shown rather than hidden.

Tiers. Tier A is scraped live from a named public source, with the URL as the receipt. Tier B is an official published figure, credited to whoever published it. Tier C — human reports from markets the internet cannot see — is not on this page yet, and will never be blended silently into the others.

Coverage. Grocery prices here are Lagos online retail, which sits above open-market prices. That is a real limitation, stated on purpose rather than buried.

Raw data. Every day's rows go unmodified into a public archive — append-only, timestamped, never edited after the fact. Official figures are reproduced from the National Bureau of Statistics and credited to them. Public data free forever with attribution.
